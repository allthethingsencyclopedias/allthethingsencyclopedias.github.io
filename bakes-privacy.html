AllTheBakes — Privacy Policy

by AllTheThings (Miniboss Studios Oy)

Last updated: 6 September 2026

AllTheBakes (“the app”) is published by Miniboss Studios Oy, operating as AllTheThings (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app does and does not handle. Contact: allthethingsencyclopedias@gmail.com.

The short version. AllTheBakes is an offline recipe app. It has no accounts, no login, and makes no network calls of its own. The recipes, your favourites, and your settings all live on your device. We do not have a server and we never receive your personal data. The only data collection happens through third-party services used in the free, ad-supported version: Google AdMob (ads) and, if you buy “Remove ads”, Google Play Billing via RevenueCat (purchases).

What the app itself stores

  • On your device only: your favourites, theme choice, autoplay settings, the Pick Random preference, and whether you have removed ads. This never leaves your device and we cannot see it.
  • The app requests no location, contacts, camera, microphone, or file permissions.

Advertising (free version) — Google AdMob

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To do this, Google may collect and process, on its own behalf, a device advertising identifier, IP address and coarse location derived from it, and device and usage information (device type, OS, ad interactions), to serve and measure ads and, if you consent, to personalise them. In the EU/EEA/UK the app shows a consent screen (Google’s UMP) before any personalised ads; you can choose non-personalised ads. See Google’s policies: google.com/technologies/ads and google.com/privacy.

Buying Remove ads stops the app requesting or showing ads.

Purchases — Google Play Billing & RevenueCat

If you buy “Remove ads”, the transaction is handled by Google Play Billing, and we use RevenueCat to validate the purchase and remember your entitlement. These services process purchase tokens and a Play-provided app-instance identifier; we never receive your card details or full payment information. See RevenueCat’s policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children

The app is suitable for a general audience, but it is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from children. The advertising described above is the reason for that distinction, not the content.

Allergens and recipe content

The recipes are provided for general information. Many contain common allergens including gluten, dairy, egg and nuts, and we make no dietary, nutritional or substitution claims. Please check ingredients yourself if you are baking for someone with an allergy.

Your rights (GDPR / EU)

Because we hold no personal data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side. For data processed by the third parties above, exercise your rights and manage consent through:

  • your device’s advertising ID / “Ads” settings (reset or opt out of ad personalisation);
  • the in-app consent screen (EU/EEA/UK), re-openable from Settings;
  • Google and RevenueCat directly, per their policies linked above.

Data sharing & selling

We do not sell your data. We do not share data with anyone beyond the ad and payment providers strictly needed to run the free ads and the optional purchase.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change. Material changes will be noted in the app’s store listing.
